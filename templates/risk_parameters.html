Understanding Risk Parameters

Vault Balance - The default account balance used for calculating position sizes.

Risk Per Trade - The percentage of your vault balance that you're willing to risk on a single trade.

For example, with a vault balance of $1000 and a risk of 0.3% (0.003), the maximum you would risk on a single trade is $3.

Lower risk percentages lead to smaller position sizes but limit potential losses.
