Understanding Signal Parameters
Fibonacci Levels

Fibonacci retracement levels are used to identify potential support and resistance areas. The default values are the standard Fibonacci ratios (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 78.6%).

ATR Multipliers

ATR (Average True Range) is used to determine volatility-based stop loss and take profit levels. Higher multipliers mean wider stops and larger profit targets.

Price Percentiles

The counter-trend percentile determines how extreme price must be before generating a counter-trend signal. A value of 0.85 means price must be in the top 15% of the recent range.

Support & Resistance

These parameters control how the system identifies and uses support and resistance levels. Setting minimum distance prevents clustering of levels, while maximum distance ensures levels are relevant to current price action.
